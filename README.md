# 📱 Smartphone_Market_Intelligence_Dashboard_in_Tableau

A comprehensive data analysis and interactive visualization project focused on smartphone market trends, pricing segment breakdowns, hardware performance metrics, and manufacturer positioning built using Tableau and Python.
---
## 📌 Project Objective
The primary objective of this project is to analyze market distribution, spec density, hardware configurations, and value propositions across global smartphone manufacturers using detailed product specification data. The insights generated aim to help consumers, market analysts, and *product strategists understand:
 Overall pricing dynamics and segment distributions across device tiers.
 Market share distribution among dominant smartphone brands (OEMs).
 Hardware specification trends, including RAM capacity and processor silicon distribution.
 Performance-to-price efficiency (Value Index) across Budget, Mid-Range, and Premium categories.
 ---
# 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Questions Addressed
---
## 📈 Core Metrics (KPI Cards)
 Total Analyzed Devices: 952 registered smartphone models.
 Overall Median Price: ₹23,990 across all market tiers.
 Overall Average Price: ₹32,485.
 Top Value Smartphone (Budget Segment): Realme C25 / Realme C2S (Value Index: 180.1).
 ---
## ❓ Analytical Questions Addressed
* Brand Concentration: Which manufacturers dominate total device counts and maintain the highest average specification scores?
* Segment Breakdown: What is the distribution of market offerings across Budget, Mid-Range, and Premium price segments?
* Hardware Profiling: What are the most common RAM capacity configurations and processor vendor market shares?
* Price vs. Performance Correlation: How do specification scores scale with pricing, and which devices deliver maximum spec density per rupee spent?
---
## 💡 Key Project Insights
* Brand Market Distribution:
Samsung leads volume representation with 14.01% (133 models), followed closely by Realme (129 models), Vivo (109 models), Xiaomi (8.96%), and Oppo (71 models).
* Mid-Range Dominance:
The Mid-Range tier forms the largest share of market inventory with 437 models, followed by the Premium tier (266 models) and Budget tier (249 models).
* Hardware & RAM Standards:
8 GB RAM is the dominant memory configuration across devices (437 models), with 12 GB RAM holding second place (238 models) and 4 GB RAM serving as the entry-level baseline (117 models).
* Silicon Architecture Share:
Qualcomm Snapdragon (365 models) and MediaTek Dimensity (349 models) control over 75% of the processor market share across cataloged devices.
* Value Benchmarking:
While specification scores plateau near 90–100 points in high-end flagship devices (e.g., Sony Xperia 1 VII, Apple iPhone series), budget models like Realme C25/C2S achieve exceptional value efficiency scores (180.1 index) due to balanced hardware specs at minimal entry costs.
